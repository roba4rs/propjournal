Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At WNF Journal, we prioritize the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by our application and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

If you choose to log in using Google Authentication, we receive your publicly shared profile information provided by the identity provider (such as your name, email address, and profile picture URL).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to manage your user account, authenticate your sessions, personalize your dashboard, and provide the core data-logging functionalities of the application. We do not sell, trade, rental, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Security and Infrastructure

We implement standard security protocols to ensure your data remains safe. Authentication tokens and user data are securely processed and stored via our backend service provider, Supabase.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your registered support channels.
